A government-funded project at the [Vision Lab, IIIT Delhi](https://www.iiitd.ac.in/), advised by Dr. Saket Anand, titled *"Automated Road Inspection using Videos for E-Marg."*

Key contributions:

- Fine-tuned state-of-the-art vision models — DeepLabV3+, SAM, and EfficientNet — for small-scale datasets, developing an integrated model that improved Road Quality Assessment accuracy by 6%.
- Researched novel Active Learning (AL) methods and frame selection strategies for video modalities, aimed at significantly reducing annotation costs for large-scale raw video data.
