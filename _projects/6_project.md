Selected for the **Microsoft Codess Program** (2020), a competitive initiative to empower women in software engineering, I designed and implemented a mini Mars Rover navigation system.

- Implemented core AI path planning algorithms: A*, Dijkstra's, DFS, and Bidirectional BFS.
- Focused on ethical AI principles throughout the design.
- The project led to a direct internship offer for Summer 2021.
