At **IHub-Data, IIIT Hyderabad**, I worked with Dr. UD Priyakumar in computational drug design for de novo drug molecular generation.

- Extended state-of-the-art baseline models for multi-property prediction on the large-scale Harvard Therapeutics Data Commons (TDC) ADMET dataset.
- Contributed to designing multiple GNN encoder-decoder architectures using Graph Isomorphism Networks (GINs), Graph Attention Networks (GATs), and Graph Convolutional Networks (GCNs).
