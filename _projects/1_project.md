**SeamFormer** is a text line segmentation model for handwritten and historical documents, developed as part of my Master's thesis with the IHDIA research group at [CVIT, IIIT Hyderabad](https://cvit.iiit.ac.in/), advised by Dr. Ravi Kiran S {% cite vadlamudi2023seamformer %}.

The model was later extended and deployed into the [Bhashini](https://bhashini.gov.in/) platform — India's national language technology mission — to support processing of ancient Indic palm leaf manuscripts for a community of 1000+ users and academicians.

Follow-up work, **LineTR** {% cite vadlamudi2024linetr %}, pushed this further toward a unified segmentation approach for particularly challenging palm leaf manuscripts.
